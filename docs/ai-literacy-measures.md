# AI literacy measures (EU AI Act Article 4)

Article 4, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, asks providers and deployers of AI systems to "take measures to support the development of AI literacy" of their staff. This log records what we did, when, and why. It names no individuals.

| Date       | Measure                                                 | Why (evidence)                                                     | Who (team, number of people) | Follow-up                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 2026-10-22 | Guardrail report set up (`npm run -s guardrail:report`) | IT security asked how we know the guardrails work                  | Pilot owner, 1               | Review the first full week with IT security |
| 2026-10-15 | Pilot notice drafted (`docs/pilot-telemetry.md`)        | The pilot's telemetry has to be explained before it is switched on | Pilot owner, 1               | Send to HR, the union contact and the DPO   |

When the guardrail report shows the same reason many times in a week, add a row: what you changed (the rule, or a 15-minute walkthrough of why it exists), the week and count that prompted it, and which team took part.
